# Code of Conduct

yamlreel is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly place to ask questions and contribute.

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Welcome newcomers. Answer questions patiently and explain your reasoning in reviews.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- No sexualised language or imagery, and no publishing other people's private information.
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests.

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where you represent the project.
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and lock or close threads, that break these rules, and may ban repeat offenders.

To report a problem, you can use the private reporting form on this repository's Security tab ("Report a vulnerability"), which also works for conduct reports,
or contact the owner, [@nsijwali](https://github.com/nsijwali), through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
privately and in good faith.
